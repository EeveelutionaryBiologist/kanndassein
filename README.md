## Claude has opinions

Have you also ever found that your Agent refused to lay out the facts and call an idea dumb when it is? 
Well, fret not, for this Skill fixes that. 

When you suggest something that is so unreasonable as to border on the insane, Claude will tell you what it thinks. Examples include, but are not limited to, intentionally self-destructive dietary choices, getting overleveraged in volatile stock options or telling your boss what you really think of them.
